Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

In short
  • You can use the whole site without an account and without giving any personal information.
  • There are no cookies, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Saved sets stay in your own browser. They are never sent to me.
  • The only time you send me anything is if you choose to report a problem or email me.

Who I am

Maths Problem Solving (mathsproblemsolving.uk) is run by Zain Husayni, an individual based in the United Kingdom. I am the data controller for the information described here. You can contact me at my email address.

What I collect, and why

When What Why, and the lawful basis
You report a problem with a question Your message, the question and its mark scheme, and your email address if you choose to give one. To fix the question, and to reply to you if you asked. Lawful basis: my legitimate interest in keeping the questions correct, and your consent for the optional email address.
You email me, for example about tuition Your email address and whatever you write. To reply to you. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, or steps towards a contract if you ask about tuition.

I do not sell your information, use it for marketing, or share it with anyone except the services listed below that make the site work.

Please do not put personal details, such as a student's full name, in a problem report. The report box is for describing what is wrong with a question.

Information that stays on your device

If you save a set, it is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to me or anyone else. You can delete a saved set in the builder, or clear all of them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. This is not a cookie and is not used to track you.

Services the site relies on

Service What it does What it receives
GitHub Pages Hosts the site. Like any web host, your IP address and browser details when you load a page, which GitHub may log for security. I cannot see these logs.
Web3Forms Passes problem reports to my email, as the site has no server of its own. The contents of a report and your IP address. Web3Forms says it does not store the contents of submissions and keeps server logs for a short period. Its servers are in the United States.
Google (Gmail) My email inbox. Problem reports and any email you send me.

Some of these services are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on the safeguards those services provide, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

The "Buy me a coffee" link takes you to a separate site with its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to it unless you click the link.

How long I keep it

I keep problem reports and emails only as long as I need to deal with them, and delete them within 12 months. If you become a tuition client, I keep what is needed for that arrangement for as long as it lasts and as the law requires.

Children

The site is for students as well as teachers, and nobody needs to give personal information to use it. If you are under 13, please ask a parent, carer or teacher before sending a report with your email address.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the information I hold about you, and ask me to correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can also object to how I use it. Email me and I will reply within one month. You do not need to give a reason.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, please tell me first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

Changes

If I change how the site uses information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
